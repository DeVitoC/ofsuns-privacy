Privacy Policy: Of Suns and Sovereigns

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Of Suns and Sovereigns does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

  • Your games stay on your device. The game keeps your settings and saved games on your device only. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • No connections, no tracking. The game makes no network connections. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. It uses no third-party services that collect data.
  • Backups. If your device backs up its apps' data (for example to iCloud or to your Google account), your device's maker handles that backup under its own privacy policy.
  • Children. The game collects no information from anyone, children included.
  • Changes. If this ever changes, for example if online play is added, this policy will be updated before the change reaches you. The App Store and Google Play listings will then say what is collected and why.
